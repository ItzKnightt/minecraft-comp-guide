# Desk

A short side note, because desk setup quietly affects everything else.

- **Depth matters more than width.** You want the monitor far enough away
  (~60–80 cm) that you can see the whole screen without moving your eyes,
  and room for a large mousepad in front of it.
- **Monitor height:** top of the screen at or slightly below eye level.
  PvP is played low-sens with arm movement — a cramped desk forces wrist
  aiming and shoulder tension.
- **Clear the mousepad zone.** Anything on the pad (cables, drinks, chargers)
  changes your glide. Route mouse cables with a bungee or tape them off the
  pad edge.
- **Chair and posture:** feet flat, back supported, elbows roughly level
  with the desk. Long sessions with a bad posture cost more aim consistency
  than any gear upgrade gains.
- Stable surface: wobbly desks transfer micro-movement into your mouse arm.
