# Desk

A short side note, because a bad desk setup is a underrated disadvantage.

- You want the monitor far enough away (~60–80 cm) that you can see the 
whole screen without moving your eyes, and room for a large mousepad.
- Your eyesight should be below the top of the monitor.

- Anything on the pad (cables or pencils) can get in the way.

- Posture should be feet flat, back supported, elbows roughly level with the desk.
Long sessions with a bad posture cost more aim consistency.

- Desk should not wobble.
