# Modpacks

> **Important:** these come with ups and downsides.

## Vulkan Optimized

Insane fps, available on [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com).

Requires newer computers to use. Runs at 10x vanilla frames and 1.5x sodium
— in my experience I went from **600 → 4,000**.

## Fabulously Optimized

A great choice and my pick — doesn't degrade performance and keeps quality
good.

FPS around **500–900**.

## Adrenaline

Around **1,000** with absolutely minimal bloat.

Less graphics and other mods, but extremely high fps.
