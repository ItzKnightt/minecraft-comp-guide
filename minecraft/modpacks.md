# Modpacks

> **Important:** each of these comes with upsides and downsides — pick the
> one that matches your PC, not the one with the biggest number.

## Vulkan Optimized

Available on Modrinth. Requires a newer computer.

- ~10x vanilla FPS, ~1.5x Sodium
- Real-world result: **600 → 4,000 FPS**
- Trade-off: heavy on older hardware

## Fabulously Optimized

My pick. Doesn't degrade performance and keeps quality good.

- FPS: around **500–900**
- Trade-off: lower raw FPS than the alternatives

## Adrenaline

Extremely high FPS with absolutely minimal bloat.

- FPS: around **1,000**
- Trade-off: fewer graphics and other mods
