# Monitor

The monitor is the piece most players underbuy. It's the last thing between
the game and your eyes, and in PvP, seeing an extra frame earlier is a real
advantage.

## Spec priority

1. **Refresh rate** — 240 Hz is the sweet spot for Minecraft PvP; 360 Hz is
   the competitive upgrade if your PC can push the frames. Going from 60 to
   240 Hz is the single biggest feel upgrade you can buy.
2. **Response time / motion clarity** — fast pixel transitions matter more
   than panel type. OLED has effectively instant response and perfect
   blacks; a good fast IPS is the value option.
3. **Resolution** — 1440p is the sweet spot for 240 Hz+ Minecraft. 1080p is
   fine and cheaper to drive.
4. **Panel:** OLED (true blacks, zero smearing, but mind burn-in with
   static HUD elements) or fast IPS (cheaper, no burn-in worry).

## Settings to get right

- Run **full screen**, not borderless windowed, for lowest input lag.
- Enable **G-Sync / FreeSync** and cap your FPS a few frames below the
  monitor's max refresh — smoothness without tearing.
- Use the monitor's fastest overdrive mode (but if you see inverse-ghosting
  halos, back off one step).
- Set the monitor's refresh rate in your OS display settings — it often
  defaults to 60 Hz even on a 240 Hz panel.
- OLED care: let the panel run its pixel-refresher, don't leave a static
  HUD up for days, and dim static UI elements.

## How many FPS do you need?

A rule of thumb: your PC should hold your monitor's refresh rate *consistently*.
A 240 Hz monitor fed 180–200 FPS with frametime dips feels worse than a
stable 165. Match the monitor to what your PC can actually sustain
(see [PC](pc.html)).
