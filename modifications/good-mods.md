# Good Mods to Add

The individual mods that earn a slot in a PvP instance. All are on
Modrinth/CurseForge and install in one click from Prism.

## Performance (install first, always)

- **Sodium** — the rendering rewrite; the single biggest FPS gain.
- **Lithium** — game-logic optimizations.
- **FerriteCore** — slashes memory usage.
- **Entity Culling** — skips rendering entities you can't see (huge on
  busy servers).
- **ModernFix / Dynamic FPS** — load-time and idle-time fixes.
- **LazyDFU / C2ME** — faster startup and chunk generation where available
  for your version.

## PvP feedback & HUD

- **Hit feedback mods** — clear hitmarkers/armor indicators so you can read
  trades at speed.
- **HUD mods** — potion timers, CPS counters, and cooldown displays; know
  your own state without guessing.
- **Armor/HUD status** — durability and effect visibility at a glance.
- **Toggle-sprint** — sprint without holding a key; nearly universal
  among PvP players.

## Texture pack

> **Recommended texture pack:** <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P-zZNhVxgro>

A consistent low-fire texture pack (or PvP-oriented pack) makes hits,
blocks, and bow timings read faster. Drop the pack into the instance's
`resourcepacks` folder and enable it in Options → Resource Packs.

## Rules of thumb

- Never add a mod mid-competition without testing it in practice first.
- Check every mod against the server's allowed-mods list — HUD mods are
  usually fine; anything automating gameplay usually isn't.
- Fewer mods = fewer update-day crashes. Add only what you actually use.
