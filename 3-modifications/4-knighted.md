# Knighted

Knighted is the feature modpack — worldgen, structures, and exploration
tuned for long-term play. Where the performance packs strip Minecraft down
for combat, Knighted builds it up.

## What's inside

- **World generation** — terrain mods that reshape oceans, biomes, and
  continents for more varied exploration.
- **Structures** — structure packs (Trek / Terralith-style content) that
  add discovery to exploration; farm-heavy structure mods were deliberately
  removed to keep progression earned rather than generated.
- **Performance base** — the same Sodium-family stack, so the pretty world
  still runs well.

## Playing it well

1. **Pre-generate your world** with a chunk-pregen tool before long
   sessions — worldgen mods are chunk-cheap on the fly but chunk-expensive
   the first time.
2. **Give the JVM 6–8 GB** — feature packs hold more in memory than
   performance packs.
3. **Pick the version intentionally** — Knighted targets a specific
   version's mod ecosystem; don't mix instances.
4. Compare world-gen options before committing a world: tools like Chunky
   (pre-gen) and Voxy (streaming distant chunks) change how the pack feels
   in play more than any single biome mod.

## Knighted vs a PvP pack

Keep them in separate instances. Knighted trades FPS for content; your PvP
pack trades content for FPS. Trying to make one instance do both gives you
the worst of each.
